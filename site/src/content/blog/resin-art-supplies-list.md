Resin supply lists online fall into two traps: the 6-item list that leaves you reordering mid-project, and the 40-item list padded with affiliate links. This is the middle path — everything you need, nothing you don't, clearly tiered.

## Tier 1: Essentials (Buy First)

- **Epoxy resin 1:1, 500ml–1L** — the project itself. 1:1 ratio for easy measuring.
- **Silicone molds** — coaster set plus one tray mold. Silicone only; plastic molds stick and tear.
- **Nitrile gloves, box of 100** — you'll burn through them. Not latex.
- **Silicone mixing cups (100ml) + stir sticks** — get more cups than you think. Dried resin peels out of silicone, so they're reusable.
- **Mica powders, starter set** — more forgiving than alcohol inks for beginners; harder to overdo.
- **Long-neck lighter** — bubble removal on a budget.
- **Plastic drop cloths or silicone mat** — resin on a table is permanent. Protect everything within a meter.

## Tier 2: Safety (Buy With Tier 1, Honestly)

- **Respirator with organic vapor cartridges** — required for pours over ~200ml or any sanding. Dust masks don't count for vapors.
- **Safety glasses** — resin in the eye is an ER visit.
- **Apron or dedicated clothes** — cured resin on fabric is forever.

## Tier 3: Quality of Life (Buy After Project 3)

- **Heat gun** — faster, more even bubble removal than a lighter. Worth it once you're pouring weekly.
- **Alcohol inks** — for petri-dish effects and translucent color that mica can't do.
- **Digital scale (0.1g)** — for 2:1 resins and repeatable custom colors.
- **Wet/dry sandpaper set (400–3000 grit)** — for edge finishing and jewelry pieces.
- **Polishing compound** — restores glass clarity after sanding.

## Tier 4: Skip Until You're Selling

- **Pressure pot + compressor** — kills all bubbles, costs $200+. Justified by revenue, not enthusiasm.
- **Resin turner** — only for tumblers, a whole separate craft.
- **Vacuum chamber** — degasses resin before pouring. Nice, unnecessary for 95% of craft work.
- **UV resin + lamp** — a different medium for jewelry. Buy it when you start that craft, not before.

## The Reorder Trap

Two consumables always run out first: gloves and mixing cups. When you place your first order, double the gloves and add a 10-pack of spare cups. Your second order should be resin and pigments, not basics you forgot.

## Budget Summary

| Tier | Cost |
|---|---|
| Essentials | $30–60 |
| Safety | $25–40 |
| Quality of life | $40–70 |
| **Total to start well** | **~$100–170** |

You can start for $50 and add safety gear the same week. Don't start without the gloves.
