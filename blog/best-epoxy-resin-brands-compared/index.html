This guide compares six real epoxy resin brands by their formulation, cure times, ease of use, and typical applications. We do not test products in-house — all specs come from manufacturer data sheets and verified public sources. If you’re buying for a specific project, match the brand to your pour depth, working conditions, and budget.

What to Buy by Pour Type

Pour Type Recommended Brand Key Specs
Thin coatings & jewelry (≤¼” deep) ArtResin 1:1 by volume mix ratio, 40–45 min pot life, 24-hr tack-free, 72-hr full cure. Low VOC, UV-stabilized. Best for art pieces where clarity and non-yellowing matter.
Table tops & moderate pours (¼–½” deep) TotalBoat ThickSet 1:1 by volume, 30-min pot life, 4–6 hr demold, 5–7 day full cure. Designed for single-layer pours up to ½”. Works well for table tops and coasters.
Deep pours (≥½” deep, single layer) System Three Phase Two 2:1 by volume mix ratio, 105-min pot life at 77°F, handles pours up to 2” per layer. Formulated for deep casting without excessive exotherm.
River tables & large formats East Coast Resin 1:1 by volume, ~30-min pot life, fast leveling, marketed for river pours and large-format art. Cure profile similar to TotalBoat ThickSet.
Budget-friendly craft projects Magic Resin 1:1 by volume, ~30-min pot life, economy-grade formula. Acceptable for practice pieces but UV stability and clarity not comparable to premium brands.
Fast-detail casting (minutes, not hours) Craft Resin (Art & Craft) 1:1 by volume, 30–40 min pot life, full cure 24–36 hr. High detail reproduction, moisture-sensitive — requires dry workspace.

Troubleshooting Tips

Symptom Likely Cause Fix
Sticky surface after 48 hr Amine blush from humidity or off-ratio mix Move to dry warm room (≥25°C); sand off blush; apply fresh thin topcoat
Bubbles popping back Resin too cold or mixed too slowly Warm resin bottle in water bath (≤40°C) before mixing; stir slowly and deliberately
Cracks during cure Exotherm from overly deep single pour Pour in layers ≤ specified depth (see brand tables); exotherm risk rises sharply beyond rated depth
Soft spots, never fully hard Off-ratio mix or old hardener Scrape back to hard cure; always mix exact ratio by volume or weight per datasheet
Cloudy/milky finish Moisture exposure during cure Work in humidity < 70%; ensure mold and workspace are bone-dry before pouring

Bottom Line

Match the brand to your pour depth and application: ArtResin for thin art pieces where clarity matters; TotalBoat ThickSet for table tops and coasters; System Three Phase Two for deep single-layer casts; East Coast Resin for river tables; Magic Resin for practice; Craft Resin for fine-detail miniatures. Always mix by the brand’s specified ratio, pour within the rated depth, and allow full cure time before subjecting the piece to use or stress.
