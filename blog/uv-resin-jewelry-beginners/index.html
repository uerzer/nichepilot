UV Resin Jewelry for Beginners: Start to Finish

Make your first UV resin pendant today — supplies, layering technique, curing, and the mistakes that ruin small pieces.

Sep 14, 2026

UV resin is the fast track of the resin world: no measuring, no mixing, cured in minutes under a lamp. It’s the best entry point for jewelry-sized pieces. Here’s the complete first project.

What You Need

  • UV resin (hard type) — hard cures glass-clear and rigid; soft stays flexible for dome stickers only.
  • UV lamp 36W+ — nail lamps work perfectly. Higher wattage cures faster and deeper.
  • Open-back bezels or small silicone molds — bezels are easier for piece number one.
  • Gloves, tweezers, toothpicks — precision work needs precision tools.
  • Inclusions: dried mini flowers, glitter, foil flakes, tiny charms.

The First Pendant, Step by Step

  1. Place the bezel on a flat surface. Work on a silicone mat — drips cure rock-hard under ambient UV over time.
  2. Squeeze a thin layer of resin into the bezel, ~2mm. Spread with a toothpick.
  3. Add inclusions with tweezers. Less than you think — pieces look better minimal.
  4. Cure 2 minutes under the lamp.
  5. Add a second thin layer to seal everything. Cure 2–4 minutes.
  6. Dome: add one small drop in the center and let it self-level for a minute, then final cure 4 minutes.

The Rules That Matter

  • Thin layers only. UV light penetrates ~3mm. Thick pours cure on top and stay liquid underneath — the classic sticky-pendant failure.
  • Clear or translucent colorants only. Opaque pigments and dark mica block UV. Use translucent dye-type colorants made for UV resin.
  • Cure both sides. Flip small pieces and cure the back too, especially with opaque bezels that shadow the edges.
  • Sunlight works but slowly and unevenly. A $15 lamp beats a sunny windowsill every time.

Sticky Surface Fix

Tacky after full cure usually means under-cured (cure longer) or oxygen inhibition (thin surface layer stays tacky in air). Fixes: longer cure, a final clear dome layer, or a quick wipe with 91% alcohol after curing.

Bottom Line

Bezel, thin layer, inclusions, cure, seal, done — a finished pendant in under 30 minutes. UV resin is the lowest-commitment way to find out if you love this craft.
